PC1: Spectral Brightness & Distortion

PC1 accounts for the largest proportion of total variance. Positively loaded on Spectral Centroid, Rolloff, and Zero Crossing Rate. High values separate high-energy genres (Metal, Rock) from low-frequency genres (Classical, Jazz).

PC2: Rhythmic Pulse vs. Harmonic Timbre

Positively correlated with RMS Energy and Beat Tempo, and inversely correlated with harmonic variance. Clearly differentiates percussive genres like Disco and Hip-Hop from acoustic Country and Blues.

Manifold Topology (UMAP / t-SNE)

UMAP and t-SNE resolve non-linear manifolds: Classical forms an isolated, compact cluster due to low RMS and high dynamic range. Metal occupies the opposite extreme. Rock, Country, and Blues exhibit overlapping neighborhoods due to shared guitar timbre.
